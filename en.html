AI Super Cleaner Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 — العربية · English

Summary

AI Super Cleaner Ultimate is designed to help you understand and clean your storage safely. Indexing, analysis, cleaning, and compression run on your device. In normal operation the app does not upload your photos, videos, audio, file names, or file contents to any server operated by us.

Data processed locally by the app

After you grant the appropriate Android permissions, the app reads metadata provided by MediaStore, such as internal URI, display name, MIME type, size, modification date, media duration, and relative path when available. The app may compute SHA-256 hashes, perceptual hashes, and image-analysis scores to detect duplicates, similarity, and blur. This data is stored in a local Room database and is not uploaded to our servers.

The app stores preferences such as optional-analytics consent and settings state, and keeps Trash and compression history locally so you can restore or review items. It may also store cleaning rules, scheduling settings, storage alerts, language and theme selection, and RAM and battery information locally.

Privacy Scanner examines user-launchable installed apps and their declared permissions when you request it. The app uses narrowly scoped Android package visibility through <queries> and does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES. Scan results stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform. The app refreshes consent status on each launch, shows the consent form when required, and does not initialize AdMob or request ads before the consent flow is complete. You can change your privacy choices from the Privacy Center when UMP provides an entry point.

Google may process information such as device identifiers, ad data, approximate IP address, and diagnostics and performance data according to Google's settings, consent, and your region. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google Ads. Rewarded ads are optional, and no ad is required to delete a file.

Google Play Billing

When you buy Premium, Google Play processes the payment, subscription, or one-time purchase. The app receives the product result, purchase state, and purchase token needed for synchronization and acknowledgment from BillingClient; it does not receive your card number or sensitive payment details. To review Google's data processing, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional analytics

Optional analytics are off by default. If you enable them, they are limited to the declared product metrics and do not include file names, file contents, full paths, or file hashes.

Permissions

The app may use MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on supported Android versions after you grant all-files access, in order to scan and clean files across shared storage. It also uses MediaStore and Storage Access Framework permissions for files you select. It does not request QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES, contacts, SMS, or camera permissions in the main cleaning flow; location permission may be used temporarily for Wi-Fi security checks as required by Android. It may request notifications for storage updates and scheduling, and may use biometrics to protect the private vault.

Retention and deletion

Local metadata remains until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, and you can delete Trash items and permanently delete files from within the app. Compressed copies you export to MediaStore are stored on your device and can be deleted from the app or a file manager.

You can request deletion of local data or any account-related data by emailing the contact address in the Contact section below, and we will respond within a reasonable period. Advertising and purchase data held by Google is deleted according to Google's policies and your account controls.

Your rights and contact

You can deny permissions, decline ads, change UMP options, turn off optional analytics, and delete app data at any time.

For any question about this policy, or to request deletion of or access to your data, email us at toufik.bendjeddah@gmail.com.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when we add a service or make a material change to data processing. The new effective date will appear in the updated version.
